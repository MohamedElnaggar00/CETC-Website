About Us

The Civil Engineering Testing and Consulting Unit (CETC) is a specialized technical arm operating under the Center of Innovative Technology (CINTECH) at E-JUST. We bridge the gap between world-class academic research and industrial application by providing comprehensive testing services for construction materials, roadworks, soil, and foundations. As part of a unique Egyptian-Japanese partnership, we bring unmatched precision and innovation to the region’s construction sector.

Our Engineering Services & Disciplines

Structural Engineering

Rigorous testing on concrete materials and masonry, along with structural integrity assessments and load testing conducted to international standards.

Geotechnical Engineering

Comprehensive site investigations, expert foundation recommendations, and quality control for earthworks, including backfilling and compaction testing.

Materials Engineering

Development of optimized concrete mix designs and feasibility testing for raw materials to ensure high-performance production.

Transportation Engineering

Specialized design and planning for infrastructure, featuring asphalt surface layer testing and comprehensive bituminous material analysis.

Building Units & Calibration

Quality assurance for masonry units (blocks/interlock) and professional calibration for batching plants and hydraulic equipment.
